  • Keep service-role keys and admin secrets out of browser code.

Career & Counselling Corner

Explore possibilities; you do not need to decide your whole future in Class 10.

🔬 Science

Explore engineering, scientific research, health-related studies, computing and environmental science.

Consider your interest in experiments, mathematics, technology and scientific questions.

📊 Commerce

Explore accounting, finance, economics, business, entrepreneurship and management.

Consider your interest in money, markets, organisations and business decisions.

🌍 Humanities

Explore history, geography, languages, social sciences, design, law-related study and public service.

Consider your interest in people, society, writing and ideas.

🛠️ Vocational & skills

Explore practical training, digital skills, design, electronics, IT and other career-focused programmes.

Compare course quality, eligibility, cost and future learning options.

Questions to discuss with a trusted adult

  • Which subjects make me curious enough to learn more?
  • Which tasks do I enjoy: solving problems, making things, analysing information or helping people?
  • What are the eligibility requirements and costs of courses I am considering?
  • Can I speak with a teacher, school counsellor or someone studying in that field?

Career guidance is a starting point, not a guarantee of a particular career outcome.
