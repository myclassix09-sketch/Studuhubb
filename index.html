    How to use Studyhub effectively

    1. Read the corresponding NCERT section first.
    2. Use the chapter notes to revise the main ideas.
    3. Practise formulas and worked examples on paper.
    4. Attempt the revision questions without looking at the notes.
    5. Take a quiz and revisit any topic you found difficult.

    Studyhub is an independent learning aid, not an official NCERT or CBSE website.
