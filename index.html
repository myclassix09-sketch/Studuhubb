How to revise a chapter

A repeatable routine for Maths and Science.

📖 First: Understand

  • Read the NCERT chapter carefully.
  • Write key ideas in your own words.
  • Mark anything you cannot explain yet.

✍️ Second: Practise

  • Recall formulas without looking.
  • Solve examples independently.
  • Attempt the revision questions.

🧠 Third: Recall

  • Close the book and explain the concept.
  • Make a short list of mistakes.
  • Return to difficult questions later.

🔁 Fourth: Repeat

  • Revise after one day.
  • Revisit difficult topics after several days.
  • Mix older chapters into practice sessions.
Important: Sample papers, chapter lists and exam rules can change between sessions. Always check the official CBSE/NCERT material for your examination year.

EXPLORE YOUR OPTIONS

Your interests can guide your future.

Career decisions do not need to be made all at once. Explore subjects, learn about real work, and discuss choices with teachers, family members or a school counsellor.

Mechanical, civil, electrical, electronics, computer and other engineering fields.

Try: Explore how everyday machines work and strengthen Maths and Science.

Physics, chemistry, biology, environmental science and laboratory research.

Try: Ask questions, keep observations and practise scientific reasoning.

How to choose thoughtfully

1
Know yourself

Notice what you enjoy, what you want to improve and what problems interest you.

2
Explore the actual work

Read about daily tasks, education requirements, working conditions and opportunities.

3
Check the route

Compare subject choices, eligibility, course fees, scholarships and entrance requirements using official sources.

4
Talk it through

Discuss your ideas with a trusted adult, teacher or qualified school counsellor. You can change direction as you learn more.
